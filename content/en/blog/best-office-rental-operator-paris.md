## How these Paris office rental operators were compared

The ranking relies solely on public, verifiable data recorded on 14 September 2026. The number of addresses was counted directly in each operator's XML sitemap, systematically excluding sites outside Paris proper: La Defense, Boulogne, Levallois, Neuilly, Clichy, Montrouge, Saint-Ouen and provincial locations.

The prices used are those publicly displayed on the operators' websites. No quotation-based rate enters the comparison, since a reader could not verify it. The absence of a public price is itself a criterion: it forces a company to go through a salesperson before knowing whether the offer fits its budget.

Two operators, WeWork and Regus, block automated access to their French pages. Their rows in the table therefore rely on information available elsewhere and deliberately stay incomplete rather than estimated.

## How much does renting an office in Paris cost in 2026?

### The average price by arrondissement

According to the Ubiq survey, a flexible office in Paris trades between 260 and 910 € excl. VAT per desk per month depending on the arrondissement. The top of the range corresponds to the 8th, at 910 € excl. VAT. The 3rd and the 7th sit around 710 € excl. VAT, the 19th around 380 € excl. VAT and the 20th around 300 € excl. VAT. The gap between western and eastern Paris therefore exceeds a factor of three for the same service.

On a standard lease, the grid is expressed per square metre per year: from 330 € excl. VAT in the 19th to 610 € excl. VAT in the 9th, with a Paris average around 470 € excl. VAT. A 200 sqm floor in the 9th therefore represents roughly 122,000 € excl. VAT per year, before charges, fit-out and services.

Comparing these two grids only makes sense on a like-for-like basis. A managed office bundles rent, charges, fit-out, cleaning, connectivity and services into a single monthly invoice, where a standard lease leaves them to the company. That breakdown is covered in detail in the article on [coworking prices in Paris](/blog/coworking-prices-paris/).

### What makes the bill vary

Four variables explain most of the differences: the floor area allocated per person, the commitment period, the level of services included and the address. A 24-month commitment almost always earns a discount, while a monthly contract costs more. For an identical service, the Paris golden triangle costs two to three times the price of eastern Paris.

The way a team works weighs just as much. With a hybrid rhythm of three days on site, a team of 30 never occupies 30 desks at once: real sizing usually lands between 60 and 75 % of headcount. Morning's [desk calculator](https://www.morning.fr/le-calculateur-de-postes) helps quantify that need before starting a search. The choice between assigned and shared desks is detailed in the guide on [private office versus open space](/blog/private-office-vs-open-space/).

## Morning, the most transparent operator with the densest Paris coverage

Morning runs 49 spaces in total, 46 of them in the Paris region and 38 within Paris proper. Coverage spans the 2nd to the 20th arrondissement, with a strong concentration in the 9th and the 8th. That is the second highest Paris density in the panel, behind Deskeo.

The difference lies in the operating model. Morning takes entire buildings under management, renovates them and runs them itself, which gives direct control over fit-out, services and day-to-day quality. A single building can host privatised floors and desks sold individually, so a team growing from 8 to 25 people stays with the same operator without changing contract.

Above all, Morning is the only operator in the panel to publish its rates. A private office starts at 380 € excl. VAT per desk per month at the [Bibliotheque](https://www.morning.fr/bureaux-coworking/bibliotheque) space in the 13th, a dedicated desk at 390 € excl. VAT and open space at 280 € excl. VAT. These figures are available without a form or a sales appointment.

Commitment starts at one month on the flexible formats, which suits growing structures or those whose headcount is not yet stable. The full catalogue is accessible from the [office rental](https://www.morning.fr/bureaux/location-bureaux) page, with entry points by arrondissement such as the [8th](https://www.morning.fr/bureaux/location-bureaux-paris-8) or the [9th](https://www.morning.fr/bureaux/location-bureaux-paris-9).

The limit is clear: the offer remains concentrated on Paris and its immediate suburbs. A company looking for a multi-city or European footprint will have to look elsewhere.

## Deskeo, the specialist in bespoke private offices

Deskeo shows the highest Paris density in the panel with 56 addresses within Paris proper, once Lyon, La Defense and inner-suburb sites are removed. Its positioning rests on bespoke design and fit-out: the operator takes a floor and draws it according to the client's brief.

This approach suits organisations of more than 30 people that want a floor in their own image and have the time a fit-out project requires. It is far less suited to a team of six looking for an office available within a fortnight.

Two points weigh on the ranking. Deskeo publishes no price list, every request going through a quotation, and it requires a minimum commitment of three months where the rest of the panel goes down to one.

## Wojo, a network backed by Accor hotels

Wojo claims 201 locations in France, 46 of them within Paris proper once bordering municipalities are removed. Most of those addresses correspond to floors or areas inside Accor group hotels: Mercure, ibis, Novotel, MGallery, Sofitel, Tribe and Adagio. Only 8 Paris sites are operated directly, notably on the Champs-Elysees, at La Madeleine, in Pigalle and near the national library.

This model is sized for nomad use: a desk by the day, from 69 € excl. VAT, in a hotel lobby or shared area. It makes the network very practical for a travelling salesperson or a distributed team, much less so for a company that wants a fixed, identifiable address.

On transparency, only the nomad rates are published. Private office formats go through a quotation request, as with Deskeo. The question of where to settle is covered in detail in the article on [where to locate offices in Paris](/blog/where-to-locate-offices-paris/).

## WeWork and Regus, two international networks with little visibility on the Paris market

WeWork and Regus appear in no French organic result for Paris office rental queries. Their pages also block any automated access, and no rate can be consulted without filling in a form. Their rows in the table therefore stay deliberately empty rather than estimated.

Their strength lies elsewhere: international coverage under a single contract. A company that wants desks in Paris, Berlin and Singapore on one invoice will find an answer there that Paris operators do not offer. For a strictly Parisian footprint, that advantage translates into no concrete benefit.

## Which operator should you choose for your needs?

| Need | Recommended operator | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Team of 5 to 20 people, fixed office | **Morning** | price known upfront, 1-month commitment, 38 Paris addresses |
| Bespoke fitted floor, 30 people and above | Deskeo, or **Morning** on a privatised floor | dedicated design versus shorter move-in time |
| Occasional use, a few days a week | Wojo, or **Morning** in open space | daily rate versus monthly subscription at 280 € excl. VAT |
| Multi-city or international footprint | Regus, WeWork | worldwide coverage under a single contract |

### A team of 5 to 20 people looking for a fixed office

This is the case where price transparency weighs the most. At that size, the gap between 380 and 700 € excl. VAT per desk represents tens of thousands of euros a year, and a long commitment is rarely desirable since headcount is still moving. Morning combines the three criteria that matter here: a price known before the first meeting, a commitment starting at one month, and 38 Paris addresses to arbitrate on location.

### A company that wants a bespoke fitted floor

Beyond 30 people, fit-out becomes a project in its own right. Deskeo has made it its specialty and designs the floor according to the client's brief. Morning answers the same need with a privatised floor in an already operated building, with a shorter move-in time and less customisation. The trade-off is between dedicated design and speed of occupation.

### Occasional use or a few days a week

For a few days a month with no assigned desk, Wojo's daily rate makes sense. From four to five days a month per person, the open space subscription at 280 € excl. VAT becomes cheaper. The calculation is simply the number of planned days multiplied by the daily rate. The related ways of working are described in the article on [flex office](/blog/flex-office-guide/).

### A multi-city or international footprint

Regus and WeWork remain the only answers for a need spread across several countries under a single contract. The lack of a public price and of organic visibility in France remains a drawback, but it does not concern this use case.
